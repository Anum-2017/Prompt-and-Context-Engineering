## 🧠 Core Prompting Principles

### 1️⃣ Specificity Over Generality
Avoid vague terms — details define results.

**❌ Vague:**  
> "a nice portrait"

**✅ Better:**  
> "hyper-realistic studio portrait of a woman with soft rim lighting, 85mm lens, f/1.4, shallow depth of field"

Being specific gives the AI clearer visual guidance and produces more professional, coherent images.

---

### 2️⃣ Visual Hierarchy
Build your prompt from **core to context**:

- **Main Subject** — who or what is in focus  
- **Pose/Action** — what they’re doing  
- **Environment/Background** — where it’s happening  
- **Lighting/Mood** — how it feels or looks  
- **Style/Technical Details** — photography or artistic direction  

This structure ensures the model prioritizes the most important elements first.

---

### 3️⃣ Professional Photography Language
Use the vocabulary photographers and artists use to achieve realism and depth.

- **Camera specs:** “shot on 85mm f/1.4, bokeh background”  
- **Lighting setups:** “Rembrandt lighting, soft shadows”  
- **Art styles:** “editorial photography, fine art portrait, cinematic look”  

The closer your description is to how professionals talk about visual scenes, the better Nano Banana understands your intent.

## 🔬 Anatomy of an Effective Prompt

| **Component**       | **Description**             | **Example** |
|----------------------|-----------------------------|--------------|
| **Subject**          | Who/what is shown           | “Young woman in traditional Japanese kimono” |
| **Pose/Action**      | What they’re doing          | “standing near a cherry blossom tree, holding an umbrella” |
| **Environment**      | Background or setting       | “in a misty garden at sunrise” |
| **Lighting**         | Mood/feel through light     | “soft morning light with pink hues” |
| **Style**            | Artistic or photographic genre | “cinematic, fine art portrait” |
| **Technical Details**| Lens, camera, focus         | “shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field” |
| **Mood/Atmosphere**  | Emotion, tone               | “peaceful, dreamy, nostalgic” |

---
