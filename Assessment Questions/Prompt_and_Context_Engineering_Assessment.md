# 🧠 Prompt & Context Engineering — Assessment with Answers

This document is designed to test and reinforce your understanding of **Prompt Engineering** and **Context Engineering** — two essential skills for building effective AI systems and agents.

---

## 🧩 Section 1: Conceptual Understanding

### 1. What is Prompt Engineering and why is it important?
**Answer:**  
Prompt Engineering is the process of designing, structuring, and refining the text or instructions given to an AI model to produce accurate and relevant outputs. It’s important because the AI’s response quality depends on how well the task is communicated.

---

### 2. Difference between Prompt Engineering and Context Engineering
**Answer:**  
- **Prompt Engineering:** Focuses on crafting *specific instructions or questions*.  
- **Context Engineering:** Involves building the *entire environment* in which the AI operates (tools, data, memory, tone, goals).  
> **In short:** Prompt = one request; Context = full setup.

---

### 3. How does context influence the output of an AI model?
**Answer:**  
Context provides background and purpose, guiding the AI to produce accurate, relevant, and goal-aligned results.

---

### 4. Consequences of incomplete or unclear context
**Answer:**  
Unclear context can lead to vague, irrelevant, or incorrect outputs and user frustration.

---

### 5. Define few-shot and zero-shot prompting
**Answer:**  
- **Zero-shot:** No examples given.  
  *Example:* “Translate this into French.”  
- **Few-shot:** A few examples provided before the task.  
  *Example:*  
  English: Hello → French: Bonjour  
  English: Good morning → French: Bonjour  
  English: How are you? →

---

### 6. Role of temperature in AI outputs
**Answer:**  
Controls creativity and randomness.  
- Low (0.2): Consistent, focused.  
- High (0.8): Creative, varied.

---

### 7. Importance of token limit
**Answer:**  
Token limits define how much the model can process. Exceeding them can truncate or ignore context, reducing coherence.

---

### 8. Difference between system messages and user prompts
**Answer:**  
- **System messages:** Define AI’s role/behavior.  
- **User prompts:** Provide task-specific instructions.

---

### 9. Improving poor AI responses through prompt iteration
**Answer:**  
Rephrase unclear parts, add examples, specify tone or role, and include relevant context.

---

### 10. Real-world example of effective Prompt Engineering
**Answer:**  
In customer support, a well-structured prompt ensures polite, clear, and solution-focused replies.

---

## ⚙️ Section 2: Applied & Scenario-Based Questions

### 11. AI Tutor Prompt Example
**Answer:**  
“You are a friendly science tutor for kids aged 8–12. Explain photosynthesis in a fun and simple way using examples like plants and sunlight.”

---

### 12. Re-engineered prompt for professional email
**Answer:**  
Original: “Write a thank-you email.”  
Improved: “Write a formal thank-you email to my manager for approving my leave, using a professional tone and concise language.”

---

### 13. If AI gives irrelevant answers — check or change
**Answer:**  
1. Add or clarify context.  
2. Simplify wording.  
3. Lower temperature.

---

### 14. Context for AI Interview Coach
**Answer:**  
- Role and tone (supportive, realistic)  
- Job type  
- Memory for feedback tracking  
- Clear goal: simulate real interviews

---

### 15. System prompt for research summarization
**Answer:**  
“You are a scientific summarization assistant. Summarize research papers accurately and neutrally, focusing on objectives, methods, and findings.”

---

### 16. Tool integration example
**Answer:**  
Integrating web search improves factual accuracy by providing up-to-date information.

---

### 17. Memory vs. static context
**Answer:**  
- **Memory:** Stores past interactions.  
- **Static context:** Single-session info.  
**Advantage:** Personalization.  
**Challenge:** Data privacy and relevance.

---

### 18. Resume-writing prompt example
**Answer:**  
“You are a career advisor. Improve my resume bullet points to sound more professional and results-oriented.”

---

### 19. Creating varied story endings
**Answer:**  
Increase temperature or instruct explicitly:  
“Generate three creative endings — happy, mysterious, and funny.”

---

### 20. Why Context Engineering is essential
**Answer:**  
It enables consistent, safe, goal-driven AI behavior by defining the environment, personality, and purpose behind responses.

---

## 💡 Section 3: Reflection & Critical Thinking

### 21. Reflection on poor AI answer
**Answer:**  
Likely due to unclear prompt or missing details. Clarifying the goal and constraints improves accuracy.

---

### 22. How Context Engineering ensures safety & relevance
**Answer:**  
It uses guardrails, tone settings, and verified data sources to keep outputs ethical, accurate, and aligned.

---

### 23. Future skills for Context Engineers
**Answer:**  
- Systems thinking  
- AI model comprehension  
- Ethical design  
- API/tool integration  
- Prompt evaluation

---

### 24. Impact of Context Engineering on AI’s future
**Answer:**  
It will lead to more autonomous, intelligent, and human-aligned AI systems capable of reasoning and multi-agent collaboration.

---

### 25. Prompt vs. Context Engineering
**Answer:**  
| Aspect | Prompt Engineering | Context Engineering |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Focus | Single instruction | Entire environment |
| Scope | Task-specific | System-level |
| Goal | Immediate response | Consistent, intelligent behavior |
| Analogy | Asking a question | Designing the classroom |

---

## 🧾 Summary

**Prompt Engineering** is about *asking effectively.*  
**Context Engineering** is about *designing intelligently.*

Together, they form the foundation of the next generation of AI agents — reliable, safe, and context-aware.
