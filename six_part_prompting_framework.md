## 🚀 The 6 Essential Steps

### 1️⃣ COMMAND – Start Strong
**Purpose:** Tell AI *exactly* what to do.  

✅ **Example:**  
> “Recommend a diversified investment strategy for a moderate-risk investor saving for a home in 5 years.”

❌ **Avoid:**  
> “Can you help me invest?”

**Tips:**
- Start with action verbs: `analyze`, `design`, `create`, `recommend`, `summarize`, `evaluate`
- Avoid weak verbs: `give`, `help`, `tell me`
- Set a confident, specific tone

---

### 2️⃣ CONTEXT – Provide Background
**Purpose:** Give the AI the information it needs to tailor its answer.  

Use the **Rule of Three: Who, What, When**

| Element | Description | Example |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Who** | Role, background, or situation | 32-year-old software engineer |
| **What** | Goal or problem | Saving for a home |
| **When** | Timeframe or urgency | Within 5 years |

**Pro Tip:**  
The more complex the task → the more context you provide.

---

### 3️⃣ LOGIC – Define Output Structure
**Purpose:** Guide the AI’s reasoning and response layout.  

✅ Example:  
> “List asset categories, explain the percentage allocation, and justify each choice.”

**Output Options:**
- Tables  
- Checklists  
- Numbered lists  
- Charts  
- Summaries  

**Ask Yourself:**
- What format will help me use this answer immediately?  
- Do I want something shareable, editable, or summarized?

---

### 4️⃣ ROLEPLAY – Assign an Expert Identity
**Purpose:** Make AI think and respond *like an expert.*  

✅ Example:  
> “You are a certified financial advisor with 15 years of experience specializing in personal finance.”

**More Examples:**
- “You are a senior marketing strategist focused on social media growth.”  
- “You are a software architect experienced in scalable cloud systems.”  
- “You are a legal expert specializing in contract law.”

**Tone Modifiers:**
- “Be brutally honest.”  
- “Be innovative and creative.”  
- “Focus on practical and actionable insights.”

---

### 5️⃣ FORMATTING – Organize for Clarity
**Purpose:** Structure information for readability and quick action.  

✅ Example Format:
```
1. Executive Summary  
2. Key Recommendations  
3. Action Steps  
4. Risk & Considerations  
5. Conclusion
```

**Popular Structures:**
- Numbered lists (step-by-step)  
- Bullet points (equal weight)  
- Tables (comparisons)  
- Section headers (complex topics)  
- Summary boxes (takeaways)

---

### 6️⃣ QUESTIONS – The Secret Ingredient
**Purpose:** Let AI refine the prompt by finding missing details.  

✅ End your prompt with:  
> “Ask me 10 questions that will help you tailor this strategy even more.”

**Why It Works:**
- AI identifies gaps you missed  
- Produces more accurate, customized answers  
- Builds collaboration and context refinement

**Advanced Question Rounds:**
1. Round 1 – Ask 10 questions to tailor the prompt  
2. Round 2 – Ask 10 more based on your answers  
3. Stop when AI repeats or runs out of unique questions

## 🧩 Complete Prompt Example
```
**COMMAND:**  
Recommend a comprehensive investment strategy.

**CONTEXT:**  
For a 32-year-old software engineer with moderate risk tolerance, earning $90,000/year, with $25,000 savings, and aiming to buy a home in 5 years.

**LOGIC:**  
List asset categories, show % allocation, justify each, and include example funds.

**ROLEPLAY:**  
You are a certified financial advisor with 15 years of experience in personal finance and mid-term investments.

**FORMATTING:**  
Use sections:
1. Executive Summary  
2. Asset Allocation Table  
3. Specific Investment Recommendations  
4. Timeline & Milestones  
5. Risk Management Considerations

**QUESTIONS:**  
Ask me 10 questions that will help you tailor this strategy even more.
```

## 💡 Pro Tips
- Use **strong commands + rich context** for high-quality responses.  
- For small tasks: 3 parts = `Command + Context + Logic`.  
- For big projects: all 6 parts + iterative question rounds.  
- Use **voice memos** to answer AI’s follow-ups naturally.  
- Iterate until AI stops asking new questions (that means it’s optimized).

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
1. Starting with a vague question  
2. Giving little or no context  
3. Ignoring output structure  
4. Using generic roles like “You are an expert”  
5. Stopping after the first AI reply
